What counts as a pressure ball

A ball is a pressure ball when its possible outcomes (dot, single, boundary, wicket) can swing the batting side's chance of winning by at least 1.5 times as much as an average ball. Players are scored on those balls against an average player facing the same balls.

What firm, likely and near-tie mean

How often a pick stays in the top 10 when the matches are resampled 300 times: firm 75%+, likely 50-75%, near-tie under 50%. The share in each header is how much of the rating comes from pressure balls; the rest is the player's usual form, which steadies small samples.
